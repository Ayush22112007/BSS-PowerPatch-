Transformer Oil Leakage Is More Than a Small Problem

EVERY DROP OF OIL LEAKAGE IS A WARNING SIGN

Transformer oil leakage is not merely a maintenance concern. It is often the first indication of insulation deterioration, overheating, and hidden operational risk. A minor leak ignored today can escalate into major equipment damage, emergency shutdowns, fire hazards, and significant financial losses.

  • ⚠️ Small leaks can evolve into major transformer failures
  • ⚠️ Falling oil levels weaken insulation performance and reliability
  • ⚠️ Leakage can trigger overheating, flashovers, and fire hazards
  • ⚠️ Continuous seepage accelerates corrosion of tanks and flanges
  • ⚠️ Delayed repairs increase downtime, maintenance costs, and production losses
  • ⚠️ Oil spills create environmental and compliance risks
